3. UNIQUE SELLING PROPOSITION (USP) — WHY IT BEATS CHATGPT & CLAUDE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Autonomous Agency vs Advice Hallucination:
   - ChatGPT / Claude: Dictates choices ("You should take this job because..."), 
     creating passive humans and reinforcing confirmation bias.
   - Chitragupta.AI: NEVER gives an answer. It deconstructs your thoughts so 
     YOU make the best decision.

2. 4-Step Cognitive Continuity:
   - Separates user input into:
     [1] What you said (Fact)
     [2] What you deduced (Interpretation)
     [3] What you are assuming without proof (Assumption)
     [4] Let's check that together (Verification)

3. Proactive Blind Spot Discovery:
   - Surfaces critical variables the user never thought to ask about 
     (e.g., academic attendance rules, burnout, mentor availability).

4. Counterfactual "Change One Thing" Simulator:
   - Tests sensitivity: "If the stipend were cut in half, but direct 1-on-1 mentorship 
     was offered, would you still take it?" Isolates true underlying anchor priorities.

5. Pre-Decision Checklist:
   - Turns vague anxiety into 4 concrete, actionable verification tasks before 
     contract signing.

6. Judgment-Free Tone:
   - Scores reasoning breadth and coverage, never grading or shaming human life choices. 
     Automated tests block words like "poor", "bad", or "failure".

Q1: "Why shouldn't I just paste my decision into ChatGPT or Claude?"
Answer:
"ChatGPT and Claude suffer from Advice Hallucination and Confirmation Bias. When users 
tell ChatGPT 'I want to accept this job because it pays well', it almost always says 
'Great idea! Here is why you should accept it.' It validates their bias and tells them 
what to do. Chitragupta.AI is built on cognitive decision theory: it isolates Facts from 
Assumptions, introduces overlooked dimensions you never mentioned, runs sensitivity tests, 
and never gives an answer, preserving human agency."

Q2: "How do you handle AI hallucinations in sensitive life decisions?"
Answer:
"We employ a 3-layer safety guard:
 1. Strict JSON Schema constraints: The LLM is restricted to extracting and questioning 
    claims from the user's text rather than asserting external facts.
 2. Deterministic Cognitive Fallback: Our rule-based Python heuristic calculates evidence 
    coverage ratios and domain dimensions mathematically.
 3. Non-prescriptive inquiry prompts: The system asks questions and gives verification 
    tasks so users verify primary sources themselves."

sees a crash or error."
